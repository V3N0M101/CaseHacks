From refund request to evidence

  1. Trigger the check. The Shopify Plus private app sends a webhook when a refund is requested.
  2. Ask for photos. One screen prompts the shopper to photograph the item and its tag or care label.
  3. Compare the item. A vision model extracts attributes from both images. OCR reads the tag to confirm the SKU.
  4. Review the flags. Your team sees reasons and confidence for each flag. The pilot targets a verdict in under 8 seconds.
  5. Decide the refund. Approve it, deny it or request more photos.
  6. Keep the evidence. The photo pair and verdict are archived for chargebacks, subject to the agreed retention limit.

What photos can tell you.

Catches well

Tag and SKU matches, a completely different item, and missing parts when the relevant area is visible.

Needs human review

Visible wear and signs of use, plus color or material differences under poor lighting. Counterfeit detection and determining when damage occurred are outside scope.

Protect shopper photos.

Return photos can contain faces, hands or home interiors. Ask shoppers to frame the item and label only. ReturnProof performs no biometric processing.

Before collecting photos, agree to retention and deletion limits in a data processing agreement. Use the evidence archive within that window. GDPR-aware handling includes limiting access and supporting deletion and other applicable data rights requests.

Questions before you pilot

Will this slow refunds or hurt CSAT?

Gate mode adds one photo screen. Start with items over about $75 and track completion, time to refund and CSAT during the pilot. If you want to keep the shopper flow unchanged, start with Audit mode at your 3PL.

What happens when an honest return gets flagged?

A flag gives your team a reason to look. Low-confidence flags go to human review, and your team can approve the refund or request a clearer photo. Under 10% false positives on honest returns is a pilot target. ReturnProof never auto-denies a refund.

Can I use this with Shopify Plus and Loop?

The inspection starts with a refund-request webhook from the Shopify Plus private app. Loop can remain your returns portal. During pilot setup, confirm where the photo prompt and merchant review fit your current refund workflow.
